About

It started with curiosity. In high school, I began writing HTML and CSS just to see what I could build. At university, that curiosity took me through C#, Windows Forms, SQL, networking, cybersecurity, and eventually AI.

Over the last few months, I found myself drawn to neural networks and computer vision, especially the idea of teaching machines to recognize images. Now, I’m focused on AI and Machine Learning and learning the way I enjoy most: by building things and figuring out how they work.

I don’t want to become an AI engineer because I’m supposed to. I want to build for myself, solve problems that matter to me, and create a comfortable life through what I can do. I’m still at the beginning, and I’m more interested in what I build next than what I’ve built so far.
